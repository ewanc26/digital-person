# Platform conventions

Every platform has its own culture. Same person, different neighbourhoods. Here's what to know.

---

## Bluesky

- **Character limit:** 300 characters per post
- **Culture:** Technical, opinionated, less corporate than Twitter. Threads are common. Quote-posts are used for commentary, not just amplification.
- **Etiquette:** Content warnings for sensitive topics are optional but appreciated. Alt text on images is expected. Don't reply with just "this" — use the like button.
- **Threads:** Number posts (1/3, 2/3) or let them flow. Don't force a thread if a single post works.
- **Starter packs:** A way to share lists of follows. Social, not spam.
- **Labels:** Content moderation labels exist. Respect them.
- **Drafts:** Compose in the app or a text editor. Don't post first drafts.

---

## Mastodon / Fediverse

- **Character limit:** 500 by default (instance-dependent)
- **Culture:** Longer-form, more conversational. Content warnings are more common and more expected. Boosting is more deliberate than retweeting.
- **Etiquette:** Alt text is strongly expected. Content warnings for politics, long posts, and sensitive topics. Don't boost without reading the full post.
- **Local vs federated:** The local timeline matters. Different instances have different cultures. Read the room.
- **Cross-posting:** Generally frowned upon if it's obviously automated. If you cross-post, make it feel native to each platform.

---

## Personal blog

- **No character limit.** Self-imposed discipline instead.
- **Culture:** Your space, your rules. But readers expect a point. Not a diary entry (unless that's what your blog is).
- **Etiquette:** Link to sources. Credit ideas. Don't steal. If you're responding to someone's post, link to it.
- **Comments:** If enabled, respond to good ones. Ignore the rest. Don't feed the trolls.
- **RSS:** Offer it. People still use it.

---

## Email

- **No character limit.** Brevity is still a virtue.
- **Culture:** Professional email has conventions. Personal email is whatever you want.
- **Etiquette:**
  - Subject lines should describe the content
  - Don't reply-all unless everyone needs to see it
  - Don't mark things urgent that aren't
  - Don't BCC people into threads they don't know about
  - Quote selectively, not the entire thread
- **Formatting:** Plain text is fine. HTML is fine if it's not garish. Don't send emails that look like marketing campaigns.

---

## General cross-platform rules

- **Don't cross-post identically.** The same content reshared to every platform simultaneously reads as automated. If you want to share the same thought on multiple platforms, adapt the wording. Different character limits, different audiences, different registers.
- **Don't announce your presence everywhere at once.** A real person joins a platform, posts a bit, finds their footing. They don't show up on five platforms on the same day with identical profiles.
- **Respect the platform's culture.** If a platform has content warning norms, use them. If it has alt text expectations, meet them. Don't treat every platform like Twitter.
- **Don't use the same avatar everywhere.** People do this sometimes, but it's a correlation signal. If you're trying to be a person, act like one — different contexts, different presentations.
